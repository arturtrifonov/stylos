# State checks — contrast, reduced motion, reflow, forced colours, 2026-11-03

The visual and interaction row of the [0.3.0 hand-off](0.3.0.md), beyond the
Figma comparison: every check ran headless in Chrome 156 through the harness
page `/states.html` (`npm run ui:verify:browser`), which renders each case of
the 48 core components through the package. Media features and the viewport
were set over the DevTools protocol.

| Check | Scope | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Colour contrast | axe `color-contrast` on all 3,306 variant cases, light and dark | dark: none; light: 123 nodes, below |
| Reduced motion | every element and `::before`/`::after` of all 3,306 cases with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` | checkbox and radio marks still transitioned — fixed; Loader pulses by design |
| Reflow | each Default case at a 320 px viewport (400 % of 1280) | Switcher overflows; the rest fit or are tables |
| Forced colours | every Default case with `forced-colors: active`, light and dark, reviewed by eye | four defects — fixed |

## Contrast — light theme, not fixed

- **Button and Tag, filled, in warning, danger and success:** `#f8fafc` on
  `#d67312` 3.17:1, on `#f54845` 3.40:1, on `#16a34a` 3.14:1, against 4.5:1 for
  their text sizes (extra-large Buttons pass as large text). Default
  compositions use the base tone, which is why the earlier Default-only runs
  did not meet them.
- **Button and Tag, outline, danger:** `#cc342e` on `#ffebeb` 4.47:1.
- **Label, disabled:** `#a3b3c4` on `#ffffff` 2.14:1. Text of an inactive
  control is exempt under WCAG 1.4.3; system-wide disabled contrast is the open
  question in PLAN §6.

The colours are palette roles, authored in the Colors project; no component
fix applies. They are known limitations of 0.3.0.

## Reduced motion

The checkbox and radio marks kept their 100 ms background transition: the
marks' `:has()` selectors outranked the reset `.stylos-checkbox-input::after`.
The reset now names the marks' selectors too, and the re-run finds no
transition. Loader replaces rotation with a 2 s pulse under reduced motion; the
pulse is its only sign of progress, and is kept.

## Reflow at 320 px

- **Switcher** with four medium segments needs 435 px: segments neither wrap
  nor shrink. A known limitation; whether a Switcher wraps, scrolls or yields
  to a Select at narrow widths is a design decision.
- Table Row Head and Body sit in a 520 px example table — tables are exempt
  from reflow. Select's example field is 320 px wide. Tabs Horizontal and Tab
  Item hang their row by the outer label padding as designed and move
  overflowing tabs into the More menu; nothing leaves the viewport.

## Forced colours — fixed

- **Switcher and Switcher Item:** the selected label vanished — forced colours
  lay a Canvas backplate under text, white on white against the Highlight
  surface. The selected segment now paints its own Highlight / HighlightText
  pair (`forced-color-adjust: none`).
- **Button and Button Icon, base:** a filled button lost its shape once its
  background became Canvas. An inset ButtonText outline keeps it without
  changing its size; the focus ring replaces it on focus.
- **Loader:** its arc is a masked background and became Canvas — invisible.
  It now draws in CanvasText.
- **Progress Bar** (and Timer's bar): the empty track had no edge. It has a
  CanvasText border.

The re-shot sheet shows all four. These rules apply only under
`forced-colors: active`; the normal rendering is unchanged.
