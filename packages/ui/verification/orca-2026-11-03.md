## Causes

Five causes explain the eight confirmed failures:

1. **Flex labels split a control from its text** (N5, D3): Checkbox, Radio and
   Toggle Label, and Dropdown Item Checkbox and Toggle rows.
2. **`aria-label` duplicates a visible label** (W1, D3): Input Text, Select,
   and the checkbox and toggle inputs of Dropdown rows; the label is then not
   related to its control, so Orca reads it as stray dialog text.
3. **Alerts appear with their live region already filled** (P8), and Timer's
   container is not itself the status (P9); focus after Undo is the host's.
4. **The sort and filter buttons join the column header's name** (C2).
5. **Select suppresses its clear button's ring** (S6); the counter speaks bare
   numbers (N7) — a content decision.

The Orca debug log of the re-run is the evidence for 1 and for P3's open state.

## Fixes

Made on `claude/stylos-status-ec198e` by owner decision ("as recommended"),
for re-check in the walkthrough's second round:

1. Checkbox, Radio and Toggle Label and Text, and Dropdown Item Checkbox and
   Toggle lay the control and its text out inline. In a probe Chrome read over
   AT-SPI — Orca's own source of lines — each label is now one line
   (`[obj]Standard`, `[obj]Receive updates`), where it was two (`[obj]`,
   `Standard`). Every decorative pseudo-element carries `content: "" / ""`.
2. Input Text, Text Area, Input Search and Select name themselves from the
   label text by `aria-labelledby` (Label's new `label text id`); Dropdown
   rows name their input from the label text (`label ids` on Checkbox, Radio
   and Toggle Input). No field repeats its visible label in `aria-label`.
3. Alert Inline, Popup and Popup Timer stay in the DOM while closed, as empty,
   visually hidden live regions. Timer's section is its live region; its
   counter is `aria-live="off"`. The starting-set example returns focus to
   Show notifications after Close and Undo, as a host does.
4. A header with sort or filter buttons is named by its text alone.
5. Select's clear button keeps Button Inner's ring; the field shows no focus
   meanwhile.
6. Text Area describes its counter in words ("13 of 40 characters";
   `counter accessible text`).
7. Drawer's content region is a tab stop only while it holds nothing focusable.
